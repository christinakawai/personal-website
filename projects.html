Husky Maps

This is the project that made us WINNERS at the Claude AI Hackathon! As a student, I often struggled to find the shortest route to class. It usually took me until week 7 to finally optimize my path. That got me thinking, what if there were an app like Google Maps, but designed specifically for campus and can calculate the fastest route by cutting through building hallways instead of just walking around them? That is exactly what our team set out to build. We developed a campus navigation app that intelligently maps indoor pathways and outdoor routes to generate truly optimized walking directions. By leveraging AI-powered pathfinding and campus-specific data, we created a solution that helps students save time, avoid congestion, and navigate campus more efficiently. So what if you're running late to class? Now with Husky Maps, there should be no excuses!

Spark Life - Offline Disaster Communication Network

When disasters strike, internet access is often the first thing to go but communication is what people need most. SparkLife is a offline first disaster communication network that runs on a local WiFi hotspot (phone or Raspberry Pi). Nearby users can connect to a lightweight web app to share needs, offers, and safety updates no internet required. Built with a FastAPI backend and an offline-first frontend, SparkLife stores data locally and includes offline maps, built-in emergency resources, and an on-device FAQ assistant. It can also run a small multilingual LLM locally for offline chatbot support. SparkLife is a functional prototype designed to keep communities connected when traditional infrastructure fails.

Team Members: Katie Hsu, Rohan Pandey, Eric Ye
